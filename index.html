About

Craig Maddux writes cozy science fiction about gods bottled into machines, the forms they're required to file, and the people who listen to them anyway. Think Douglas Adams meets Becky Chambers, with more paperwork.

His Cantica stories follow Zola Khumalo, psychiatrist to the Bound, aboard an arkship that has been looking for a new home for eight hundred cycles and filing reports about it the entire time. Cantica is also a tabletop roleplaying game he's building.

By day, he makes games as Inkdrift. By other days, he makes salami in Wisconsin.
